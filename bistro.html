Good food is only part of the story.

Set within The Landmark Suites on Mwanzi Road, Landmark Bistro is a relaxed spot to meet, eat and ease out of the day. Drop in for a catch-up over coffee, sit down to a proper meal, or make an evening of it with a drink and good company.

The idea is simple: welcoming service, a menu with something for different appetites, and a terrace that lets you slow the pace without leaving Westlands.
