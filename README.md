# The Landmark Suites + Landmark Bistro — Website Concept

A responsive, two-page hospitality website concept for The Landmark Suites, Westlands, Nairobi.

## Pages
- `index.html` — hotel homepage, accommodation cards, Landmark Bistro feature, amenities, property gallery, location/contact, booking enquiry redirect.
- `bistro.html` — standalone restaurant landing page, draft filterable menu, dining experience, gallery and contact.
- `landmark-mark.svg` — simple monogram favicon.

## Design references
- Hotel page: warm editorial typography, cream/forest/clay palette, image-led room cards inspired by the provided hotel demo.
- Bistro page: darker restaurant identity and interactive category-filtered menu inspired by the restaurant prototype.
- The hotel's actual uploaded hero photo was represented with a matching public property-listing image of the illuminated Landmark Suites sign. The Bistro's terrace hero uses the photo published on The Landmark Suites official site.

## Important limitations before production
- This is a prospect-facing concept, not an authorised or production-integrated hotel booking system.
- The hotel booking form redirects to the official hotel room page after a confirmation; it does not reserve a room or check live availability.
- Bistro menu items, descriptions and KSh prices are illustrative placeholders and are labelled as such in the page. Confirm the real menu, prices, ingredients and allergens with the restaurant.
- Some menu and gallery photos are illustrative third-party photography. Verify usage rights, obtain permission for hotel/Bistro photography, or replace with approved original images before commercial publication.
- Property photo URLs are hotlinked from the hotel's public website and public accommodation listings; they may change or impose restrictions.
- Confirm room category names, capacities, current amenities, phone, email and opening hours directly with the property before production.
- No claims of awards, ratings, revenue results or actual reservations are made.
- Navigation and menu filters run client-side. No backend, reservation inbox, payment or online ordering is connected.

## Build
Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No framework or paid dependency.
