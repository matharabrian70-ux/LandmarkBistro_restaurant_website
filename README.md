# The Landmark Suites + Landmark Bistro — Website Concept

A responsive, two-page hospitality website concept for The Landmark Suites, Westlands, Nairobi.

## Pages
- `index.html` — hotel homepage, accommodation cards, Landmark Bistro feature, amenities, property gallery, location/contact, and booking enquiry redirect.
- `bistro.html` — separate restaurant page with a filterable draft menu, dining experience, gallery and contact.
- `landmark-mark.svg` — monogram favicon.

## Design
- Hotel: warm editorial look, cream/forest/clay palette, elegant type and image-led room cards inspired by the supplied hotel demo.
- Bistro: darker dining identity and interactive category-filtered menu inspired by the supplied restaurant prototype.
- Hotel hero: public property-listing image of the illuminated Landmark Suites sign.
- Bistro hero: terrace photograph hosted on the official Landmark Suites website.

## Before production
- This is a prospect-facing concept, not an authorised or production-integrated booking website.
- The hotel's date form redirects to the official hotel website after a confirmation. It does not reserve rooms or check live availability.
- Bistro menu items, descriptions, images and KSh prices are illustrative placeholders labelled in the page. Confirm items, prices, ingredients and allergens with the restaurant before publication.
- Some menu/gallery images are illustrative stock photography. Verify usage rights or replace them with approved original images before commercial launch.
- Photos linked from the hotel's site and third-party accommodation listings can change or be subject to usage restrictions; obtain permission for commercial use.
- Confirm room names, capacities, amenities, contact information and current hours directly with the property.
- No backend, live booking engine, payments, online orders or reservation inbox is connected.
- Navigation and menu filters work client-side.

## Tech
Static HTML, embedded CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No framework or paid dependency.
